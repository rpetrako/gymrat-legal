Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Gym Rat is made by Ranko Petrakovic, an individual developer in Zagreb, Croatia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Gym Rat does not collect your data. The developer has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and cannot see your workouts.

What the app stores and where

  • On your iPhone: the workouts, sets, plans, routines and settings you enter are saved on your device.
  • In your iCloud: if iCloud sync is on (Settings › iCloud in the app), the same data is copied to your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. This storage belongs to your Apple Account; Apple holds it under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple shares only anonymous sales reports with developers.

Tracking and third parties

Gym Rat contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sets, exercises and workouts in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the developer does not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. If you have a question about your data, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
