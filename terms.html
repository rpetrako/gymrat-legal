Subscription

  • Gym Rat Yearly is an auto-renewable subscription with a one-year period. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy.
  • Free trial: new subscribers get the first month free. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged when the trial ends. Each Apple Account can use the free trial once.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase (or at the end of the free trial).
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically for another year unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Managing and cancelling: open iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Family Sharing: a subscription can be shared with up to five members of your Apple family group.

Without a subscription

Your workout history stays visible, but you can't add or change entries until you subscribe again. Your data is not deleted.

Health

Gym Rat is a log for your training. It is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, and train within your limits.
